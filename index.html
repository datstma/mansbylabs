Diskward

A disk, partition and disk-image manager. Resize, move, merge and convert partitions, write and back up images, and find out why a drive won’t eject. Every change is shown as a plan before anything is written, and partition tables are backed up automatically. On macOS, its own FSKit modules mount NTFS and ext4 volumes.

macOS, Windows, Linux
Status
In development
